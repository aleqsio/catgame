How to play

  • Put one cat in each row, each column and each coloured room.
  • Cats need space: two cats can't touch, not even at a corner.
  • Hold a cell to put a cat there. Each cat dots the cells it blocks.
  • Tap a cell to dot it (no cat here), or to clear it. Drag across cells to dot many at once.
  • Every puzzle has exactly one solution, so you never need to guess.
  • Each level is bigger and needs harder logic. Kitten needs only the last free cell of a row, column or room. Easy adds cells a cat would block. Medium needs pairs of rooms that share two rows or columns. Hard and Expert need larger groups and “what if” chains.
  • Hint explains the next logical step. Reveal shows one cat. A time with either one does not count as a best.
